Avatar Arcade

Play and create talking avatars.

Pick a character and start talking. Or make your own, just by talking it through with a creative director.

Talk

Say hi, and the conversation is live. Every character has its own portrait, voice, and personality, and it listens, answers in real time, and keeps the conversation going. Talking costs coins, and you earn tickets as you play.

Create

Start with an idea: a gruff blacksmith, a bubbly tour guide, a Sherlock Holmes for today. A creative director builds the character with you, out loud: its voice, its portrait, its music. No forms to fill in. Try another version if you like, keep your favorite, and publish it to the arcade for anyone to talk to.

Earn

When other people talk to a character you made, you earn tickets. Build one people love, and it keeps earning while you're away. Spend your tickets on prizes in the Exchange.
